Three short games. Every answer is a fact already printed on Leaders.vote. Nothing here endorses a candidate, calls a race, or keeps score against anyone else.

Leaders.vote is an independent voter guide. It is not a campaign, not an endorsement, and not a site of any election authority. Play repeats facts from those pages. A finished sheet is a puzzle, not a result.

Illinois facts are from the Illinois page: the sample ballot, the retention rule, and the note on terms. Country facts are from the country list. Names are printed as they are printed there. Photographs are used under the Unsplash License. The photographer is credited on the picture.
