Sweden · Parliament · 13 September 2026

Sweden has a final count for its parliament.

The count is for the Riksdag, the national parliament. This is one election for the whole country, not a local council and not a president.

The parliament's seats are counted in 29 constituencies. Some constituencies are a whole county. Stockholm, Skåne, and Västra Götaland are split into more than one.

The map shows the 21 counties. A county's color adds the official votes from the constituencies inside it. The color is the one the Election Authority records for that party.
