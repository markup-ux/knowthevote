Know who you're voting for.

Leaders.vote is an independent, nonpartisan voter guide for the November 3, 2026 election.

Select your state to explore candidates, understand the responsibilities of each elected office, and learn about candidates' priorities when published on their official websites.

You can also enter your street address to access your state's official election-office lookup. Your address is never stored on Leaders.vote.

Independent. Informational. No endorsements. Leaders.vote is not affiliated with any government agency.

Leaders.vote is still collecting candidate information, so some entries currently show only a name.

Leaders.vote also follows elections outside the United States. A place on those maps is colored only from reported vote totals.

The census maps the Census Bureau's July 1, 2025 population estimates, the House seats the 2020 census assigned, and a health section on the same page.

The House lists each roll call in the 119th Congress, the yes and no of each member, a phone and office for the member, and the blue slips home-state senators return for a federal judge. Green is a yes. Rose is a no. Gold is on the floor list. Slip blue is a blue slip.

The gallery is photographs from Unsplash, drawn to fill the screen. Filter them by subject, or open the grid. New photographs are added as Unsplash publishes them. An election map can sit on top of a photograph.

Each state is colored by the party ahead in that state's top statewide race. The colors are the same ones used for a county on a state map. A blank state has not reported votes. This map does not call a race.
