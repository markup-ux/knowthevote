Know who you're voting for.

Leaders.vote is an independent, nonpartisan voter guide for the November 3, 2026 election. It lists candidates, offices, sample ballots, and unofficial results. No endorsements, and no tie to a government agency.

Five national averages

A year of spending, gas, a grade-point average, a vacation, and a first date. Each number is national, and the source is named on the card. A color is a label for one kind of figure. On the spending card, a darker blue is a larger share. Leaders.vote does not adjust these figures.

Each card has its own year, and the slider stops on years that were published. Dollars are what people paid then, not today’s prices.

The census maps the Census Bureau's July 1, 2025 population estimates, the House seats the 2020 census assigned, and a health section on the same page.

The House lists each roll call in the 119th Congress, the yes and no of each member, a phone and office for the member, and the blue slips home-state senators return for a federal judge. Green is a yes. Rose is a no. Gold is on the floor list. Blue is a blue slip.

The gallery is photographs from Unsplash, drawn to fill the screen. Filter them by subject, or open the grid. New photographs are added as Unsplash publishes them. An election map can sit on top of a photograph.

Each state is colored by the party ahead in that state's top statewide race. The colors are the same ones used for a county on a state map. A blank state has not reported votes. This map does not call a race.
