Bureau of Labor Statistics, Consumer Expenditure Survey. This is the average a household spent on pets in 2025, including food, supplies, medicine, services, and the veterinarian. Homes without a pet are in the average, so it is lower than what a pet-owning home spends. The bar runs from $0 to $1,500. The tick is $880, 2024. Leaders.vote also follows elections outside the United States. A place on those maps is colored only from reported vote totals.

The census maps the Census Bureau's July 1, 2025 population estimates, the House seats the 2020 census assigned, and a health section on the same page.

The House lists each roll call in the 119th Congress, the yes and no of each member, a phone and office for the member, and the blue slips home-state senators return for a federal judge. Green is a yes. Rose is a no. Gold is on the floor list. Blue is a blue slip.

The gallery is photographs from Unsplash, drawn to fill the screen. Filter them by subject, or open the grid. New photographs are added as Unsplash publishes them. An election map can sit on top of a photograph.

Leaders.vote is an independent voter guide. It is not a campaign, not an endorsement, and not a site of any state or election authority. Campaign links, where a candidate publishes them, go to that candidate. This site does not take contributions.

A written line is added only from a candidate's own site. Illinois printed names were checked against the State Board of Elections. Names on another state page are compared with that state's candidate list before they are described as checked. Photos outside Illinois come from NBC News and the Associated Press.
