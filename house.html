Taxpayer money

Each measure here names an amount of taxpayer money in its title or in the short account. The mark is the largest amount named. A paler brass mark is a smaller amount. A deeper bronze mark is a larger amount. A measure that spends money without naming an amount there is not in this list. The full text can name other amounts.

House roll calls are copied from the Clerk of the House for 2025 and 2026. The question, the description, and the counts are the Clerk's. While this page is open it asks Congress.gov for a House roll call newer than that copy. The floor list is the latest week in the House file at docs.house.gov. Office phone, room, and website are copied from the congress-legislators file for the current Congress. Portraits are public-domain photographs from the Congressional Pictorial Directory, through the congress images collection, and from the Biographical Directory when that collection has no photograph. A floor recording is the House FloorCast for the day of the roll call. The Clerk's YouTube channel posts a floor video when one is published. A short account of a measure is the Congressional Research Service summary published by GovInfo. When that short account leads with dollar amounts, the amount and the office or program named with it are copied from that summary or from the measure's text on GovInfo. The line gives the largest amounts, not every amount in the measure. The full text is that measure's text page on Congress.gov. The sponsor is the member named in the GovInfo bill status file. Bills and resolutions that were introduced are copied from that same file. The introducer, the introduction date, the title, and the latest action are the file's. Senate roll calls are copied from Voteview's record of the Senate, because the Senate site does not offer those files to this page. The question, the description, and each senator's yes or no are Voteview's. When the bill status file says the Senate passed a measure by unanimous consent, this page says so, because that action has no senator-by-senator vote.
