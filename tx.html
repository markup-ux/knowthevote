Governor

The governor signs or vetoes bills, proposes the state budget, and appoints executive officials.

Greg Abbott

Republican

Governor of Texas, running for another term.

His campaign site identifies him as governor and asks Texans to keep the state Republican. He presents public safety, affordability, and education as the themes of the campaign.

  • He says new data centers are being paused and must show they will pay their own way, supply their own power, reuse their own water, reduce electricity costs, and avoid disturbing neighborhoods.
  • He says the state made bail tougher, backed law enforcement, and focused on violent repeat offenders.

Railroad Commissioner

The railroad commissioner serves on the Railroad Commission of Texas, which regulates oil and natural gas, pipelines, natural gas utilities, LP-gas, critical natural gas infrastructure, and coal and uranium surface mining. The commission no longer regulates railroads.

Bo French

Republican

Candidate for railroad commissioner.

He says he was raised in Midland by a father in the Texas energy business, entered the industry in 1994, and worked as an energy trader. He says he is also a rancher and mineral owner and has chaired the Tarrant County Republican Party.

  • He says the Railroad Commission oversees oil and gas plus uranium, coal, and salt, and that the seat should be held by a conservative who will back the president.
  • He says he will end diversity, equity, and inclusion programs, regulation he calls overbearing, and influence from radical Islam and the Chinese Communist Party in Texas oil.
  • He says he will enforce the Legislature's ban on hostile foreign ownership of land in order to protect the Texas oil industry.

Candidate for railroad commissioner.
