Secretary of State

The secretary of state runs the duties this state assigns that office. Those duties are not the same in every state. Michigan's secretary of state site lists election services, including voter registration and polling-place lookup, along with branch and online office services.

Republican

Former Macomb County clerk, running for Michigan secretary of state.

Anthony Forlini's campaign says he is a lifelong Michigan resident, a husband, father, and grandfather, and the son of an Italian immigrant. It says he has served as a township supervisor, a state representative, and Macomb County clerk.

  • He says he would protect election integrity by removing ineligible and deceased voters from the rolls and by requiring watermarked ballots statewide.
  • He says he would stop sending voter information to outside groups and audit county clerks so election practices match across the state.
  • He says he would cut secretary of state wait times and make routine services easier to finish online or in person.
