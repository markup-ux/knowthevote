State · Vote for one

Governor

The governor signs or vetoes bills, proposes the state budget, and appoints executive officials. Minnesota law says the governor appoints state officers and employees whose selection is not otherwise provided for by law, and may remove an appointee whose term of service is not set by law.

Lisa Demuth

Republican

Speaker of the Minnesota House.

Her campaign says she was elected to the Minnesota House in 2018, served on the ROCORI School Board, and ran a small business before that. In 2025 she became Speaker, and the site says she was the first Black lawmaker and the first Republican woman in that post.

  • She says her first priority as governor would be helping families afford their lives, including lower income-tax brackets, lower car-tab fees, and an end to the state tax on Social Security benefits.
  • She says she would create a nonpartisan inspector general to look for fraud in state programs before money leaves the door.
  • She says she would back police, firefighters, and other first responders.

Her campaign says she led Hennepin County's prosecutor's office for eight years and has served in the Senate, where she highlights bipartisan bills and a law letting Medicare negotiate drug prices. She is running for governor and says she wants state government to be more accountable.

  • She says she would work to lower health care, housing, child care, and utility bills, and to build more homes.
  • She says she would root out fraud and make it easier to start a business and get a permit.
  • She says she would take on organized crime and gang violence and help local police hire and keep officers.

Senate

The winner is one of 100 votes on federal law, spending, treaties, and judges. The term is six years. The U.S. Senate has the sole power to conduct impeachment trials.

Her campaign says she is a four-time Sports Emmy winner who covered NFL games for NBC, including five Super Bowls, and left the sideline after Super Bowl LVI. She is running for the Senate on what she calls accountability and family-first leadership.

  • She says she would cosponsor a bill to deport an immigrant convicted of fraud and would seek mandatory federal prison terms for fraud.
  • She says she would push to withhold federal money from a state that will not put anti-fraud controls in place.
  • She says Minnesota's criminal-justice experiment has failed and that she would stand with victims and law enforcement.

State · Vote for one

Attorney General

The attorney general is the state's chief legal officer. Which cases the office files, and whether it prosecutes crimes, depends on that state's law. State law requires the attorney general to appear for the state in the supreme court and in federal courts when the state is directly interested, and to appear in a criminal case when a county attorney requests it and the attorney general deems it proper.

  • He says fighting fraud and protecting tax dollars would be a top priority, and that he would try to stop fraud before it starts.
  • He says seniors, families, and small businesses need a consumer-protection lawyer who will take on scammers and powerful companies.
  • He says he would enforce the law and put public safety ahead of political headlines.

State · Vote for one

Secretary of State

The secretary of state runs the duties this state assigns that office. Those duties are not the same in every state. State law makes the secretary of state the custodian of the state seal and of state records and documents that the law does not assign to another official.

His campaign says he has been a legislator, a county commissioner, a judge, and a small-business owner. He is running for secretary of state on a plan he calls Trust the Vote.

  • He says he would work with local, state, and federal partners to clean up voter rolls.
  • He says he would try to prevent fraud in public programs and make business filings and other office services more reliable.
