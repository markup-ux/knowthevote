Governor

The governor signs or vetoes bills, proposes the state budget, and appoints executive officials. The Pennsylvania Constitution vests the supreme executive power in the governor and says a lieutenant governor is chosen jointly with the governor by one vote for both offices.

Stacy Garrity

Republican

Pennsylvania treasurer and retired U.S. Army Reserve colonel

Stacy Garrity's campaign says she is the commonwealth's treasurer, a retired Army Reserve colonel who deployed three times, and a former business executive. She is running with Jason Richey and says she wants to be the next governor.

  • Her Turn Around PA plan says she would lift a ban on new drilling sites and call a special session to speed energy permits, pipelines, and power plants.
  • The same plan says she would make daily costs more affordable, reduce property taxes for families and seniors, and restore fairness and transparency in state government.
  • She says she would roll back costly mandates and pause or reform renewable-energy and efficiency mandates so the state can produce more energy.

garrityforpa.com

Josh Shapiro

Democratic

Governor of Pennsylvania

The campaign site presents Josh Shapiro as the governor and Austin Davis as lieutenant governor. It asks voters to support Shapiro for governor.

  • His campaign says that as governor he has worked on jobs and money in people's pockets, law enforcement and community groups for public safety, education funding, and protecting freedoms.
  • The site says Pennsylvania has hired hundreds of new officers and created thousands of jobs, and that children can get free breakfast and more mental-health counselors in school.
  • It says high-school graduates have more paths through work, apprenticeships, or college, and that he and Lieutenant Governor Austin Davis are running to continue that work.

Ken Krawchuk

Libertarian

Information technology entrepreneur and Libertarian candidate for governor

Ken Krawchuk's campaign says he has been a registered Libertarian since 1993 and is running for governor with John Thomas for lieutenant governor. The site says he was born in Philadelphia's Feltonville section and has lived in Abington for decades.

  • He says he would veto tax increases, use the line-item veto, phase out the personal income tax, and abolish property taxes.
  • He says he would end the state's monopoly on schooling and privatize public schools because too many students are behind in reading and math.
  • His listed program also says he would eliminate parole for violent criminals, pardon nonviolent drug offenders, and decriminalize marijuana.
