Federal · Vote for one

Senate

The winner is one of 100 votes on federal law, spending, treaties, and judges. The term is six years.

Mike Katz

Republican

Physician and former Delaware state senator

He says he has spent 30 years in Delaware treating patients, building businesses, and filing legislation in the state Senate. The campaign describes him as a physician and pediatric critical-care anesthesiologist.

  • He says he would halt wasteful spending, cut red tape, and lower energy and housing costs.
  • He says he would expand early-childhood education, line up schooling with the workforce, and close what he calls the zip-code gap.
  • He says each vote in Washington should be judged by whether it helps Delaware, including on health-care access and affordability.

drmikekatz.com · Donate

Chris Coons

Democratic

U.S. senator from Delaware

He says he grew up in Hockessin and, before the Senate, spent a decade in New Castle County government, including as county executive. He says he previously worked as an attorney at W.L. Gore & Associates.

  • He says he has fought cuts to Medicaid and to affordable health care for Delawareans.
  • He says he has secured funding for affordable housing and for protecting the state's natural areas.
  • He says he has backed bipartisan laws on housing access, children's online safety, and health-care research.

State · Vote for one

Attorney General

The attorney general is the state's chief legal officer. Which cases the office files, and whether it prosecutes crimes, depends on that state's law.

David Graham

Republican

U.S. Army veteran and retired executive

He says he is a former Army sergeant and a retired senior executive, and that he is running from outside a career in politics. He says military, business, and working life shaped his view of discipline, responsibility, and accountability.

  • He says he would bring discipline, responsibility, and accountability to Delaware's Department of Justice.
  • He says public safety would come first.

grahamofdelaware.net

Kathy Jennings

Democratic

Attorney General of Delaware

She says she was born and raised in Wilmington, graduated from the University of Delaware and Villanova University Law School, and started as a prosecutor in the Delaware Department of Justice. She says she later served as state prosecutor and chief deputy attorney general before returning to lead the office.

  • She says she is running to make Delaware safer and to make justice fair and equal.
  • She says she prosecuted domestic violence, sexual assault, and homicide cases, and that she built the office's consumer-fraud work.
  • She says that as attorney general she has pursued child predators, opioid manufacturers, corruption, and elder abuse, and is pushing stronger laws on online predators.

kathyfordelaware.com · Donate

District 1

Joseph Arminio

Republican

Candidate for Congress

The campaign says Joe Arminio was born and raised in Wilmington, earned a political-science doctorate from MIT, and has taught and done policy work tied to defense. He says he is running as an America First candidate who wants Main Street to rise.

  • He says private and public debt are national emergencies and calls for a one-time debt jubilee plus action on the federal debt.
  • He says he wants to stop endless wars, reform education, and restore what he calls constitutional government.
  • He describes the campaign as a populist alternative to an incumbent he says is continuing a failing economy and those wars.

Dr. Joe For Congress · Donate

Sarah McBride

Democratic

U.S. representative for Delaware

The campaign says Sarah McBride, a Wilmington native, represents Delaware's at-large House seat after serving the state's 1st Senate District from 2020 to 2025. In Congress she sits on Foreign Affairs and on Science, Space, and Technology and is a House Democratic deputy whip for policy.

  • She says working people are being squeezed by grocery, gas, health, housing, and energy bills, and that policy should focus on them rather than the wealthiest.
  • Her priorities include a care economy, health care, making life more affordable, and workers' rights.
  • As a state senator she highlights the Healthy Delaware Families Act, a paid family and medical leave program, and a Medicaid bill she says brought large new funding to the state.
