State · Vote for one

Governor

The governor signs or vetoes bills, proposes the state budget, and appoints executive officials.

Byron Donalds

Republican

Member of Congress for Florida's 19th District

He says he was born and raised in Brooklyn, New York, in a single-parent household, and that his mother's push toward what he calls the American Dream still guides his work. The campaign describes him as a conservative candidate for governor.

  • He says Florida should bring down costs for working families and for seniors on fixed incomes.
  • He says patients should know the price of care before treatment and that doctors should face less red tape.
  • He says the state should secure the border, keep neighborhoods safe, and stop taxpayer support he ties to illegal immigration.

byrondonalds.com

David Jolly

Democratic

Candidate for governor of Florida

His campaign says housing costs, underfunded schools, expensive health care, and climate impacts that raise insurance are what Florida families are living with. He says property insurance is the main reason many people cannot afford a place to live.

  • He says a state catastrophic fund that takes natural-disaster risk out of the private market would cut property and car insurance rates, and he supports no-interest state loans for condominium repairs.
  • He says Florida should expand Medicaid and commit to health care for residents across the state.
  • He says the state should pause construction of hyperscale data centers until protections for water and nearby communities are shown to be enough.

davidjolly.com

D

Dean Abrams

Other

Independent candidate for governor

His campaign says it takes no party, PAC, or corporate money, and that his running mate is Joe Van Vactor. He says he answers to voters rather than to the people who fund the major parties.

  • He says homesteaded homes worth up to twice a county's median should pay no property tax, and that corporations should stop buying single-family homes.
  • He says insurer profits should be capped, rate filings written in plain language, and the right to sue restored when a claim is wrongly denied.
  • He says he would ban new AI data centers and end warrantless tracking by license-plate reader networks.

deanabrams.com

C

Charles Burkett

Other

Mayor of Surfside and candidate for governor

He says he was raised in Miami Beach, has lived in Surfside since 1996, and was elected mayor of Surfside, including a 2024 reelection. He says he owns a national investment company and is running for governor.

  • He says homestead property taxes should phase out for long-time Florida homeowners, with the benefit able to pass to a spouse or children.
  • He says property and auto insurance laws should be rewritten to bring more competition into the market.
  • He says a data center should be allowed only after voters in that county and city approve it.

burkettforgov.com

J

Jeffrey Datto

Other

Scientist and no-party candidate for governor.

Jeffrey Datto's campaign says he is a Hialeah scientist with a doctorate in cell and tissue engineering who researched paralysis recovery at the University of Miami and founded DattoAI. It says he is running with no party affiliation after experiencing illness, disability, and homelessness, and that family and faith are central to the campaign.

  • He says he would move toward universal, income-based health care, strengthen public hospitals and clinics, and create a Florida Institutes of Health focused on serious disease, injury, mental health, and longevity.
  • He says he would abolish charter schools and bring them into one state public-education system, with academic freedom and pathways from school into Florida jobs.
  • He says he would open a public-accountability office, require timely and accessible courts, and make renters share in property-tax relief given to landlords.

Dr. Jeff Datto for Governor

M

Moliere Dimanche

Other

Candidate for governor of Florida

He says he was born Moliere Dimanche, is a first-generation American, and grew up in Orlando, including in Pine Hills. He says that as a teenager he worked in grocery and restaurant jobs and later in flooring and remodeling.

  • He says unincorporated communities should be able to form their own cities so residents control safety, taxes, and public works.
  • He says he would close Alligator Alcatraz on his first day in office.
  • He says he would abolish homeowners associations and end property taxes on homesteaded homes.

F

Frank Russo

Other

Businessman and insurance executive

He describes himself as a Florida-born businessman, entrepreneur, insurance executive, and developer running for governor, with Rachel Rodriguez as his running mate. He says many people who already live in Florida are being priced out as the state grows.

  • He says an affordability plan should take on insurance, housing, property taxes, and everyday costs together and protect homeowners.
  • He says he wants Florida's average teacher salary raised to at least $75,000, with more skilled-trades and apprenticeship paths.
  • He says lawful medical decisions should be made by patients and their doctors, and that immigration enforcement should focus on violent criminals, traffickers, and gangs while supporting legal immigration.

russo2026.com

Scott Jewett

Libertarian

Entrepreneur and Libertarian candidate for governor

He says he is a Boca Raton entrepreneur running with his wife, Regina, and that they live with homeownership and rising insurance costs. He says he is running because Florida can do better with less government and more individual liberty.

  • He says he would take on the insurance affordability crisis, cut taxes, and shrink government so families keep more of what they earn.
  • He says data centers should do no harm and that the state should stop using Flock license-plate cameras.
  • He says parents should decide where their children go to school and that education dollars should follow the child.

Senate

The winner is one of 100 votes on federal law, spending, treaties, and judges. The term is six years.

Ashley Moody

Republican

U.S. senator and former Florida attorney general

She says she is a fifth-generation Floridian raised in Plant City who earned degrees from the University of Florida and a master of laws from Stetson. She says she was a federal prosecutor and a circuit judge before her election as attorney general, and that she was sworn into the Senate on January 21, 2025, after an appointment by Governor Ron DeSantis.

  • She says she supports America First policies on law and order, economic prosperity, and American sovereignty.
  • She says that as attorney general she championed public safety, support for law enforcement, and efforts against violent crime.
  • She says that as a senator she is working to uphold conservative values and strengthen national security.

ashleymoody.com · Donate

Angie Nixon

Democratic

Florida state representative and union organizer

She says she is a state representative, union organizer, and mother who was born and raised in Jacksonville. She says she started as an organizer in Duval County and ran for office because she wanted more working people in government.

  • She says she is running to lower costs, raise wages, and put working families ahead of billionaires.
  • She says she supports Medicare for All and a national rent freeze with a moratorium on evictions.
  • She says she supports free child care and pre-K, and a Working Families Guarantee for workers.

The attorney general is the state's chief legal officer. Which cases the office files, and whether it prosecutes crimes, depends on that state's law.

He says he previously served as Governor Ron DeSantis's chief of staff and, before that, as counsel to Donald Trump's 2016 campaign and as a senior advisor to Commerce Secretary Wilbur Ross. He says he led state legal fights to reopen businesses and schools and to prohibit mask and vaccine requirements.

  • He says his work with the governor included education freedom, parental rights, election rules, tax relief, and public safety.
