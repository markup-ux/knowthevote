Governor

The governor signs or vetoes bills, proposes the state budget, and appoints executive officials. Wisconsin's constitution says the governor and lieutenant governor are chosen jointly, by one vote that applies to both offices.

Tom Tiffany

Republican

Member of Congress and former small-business owner running for governor.

His campaign says he grew up on a dairy farm in western Wisconsin, worked his way through UW-River Falls, and with his wife ran Wilderness Cruises in the Northwoods for two decades. It says he also tended a dam on the Willow Flowage for 29 years.

  • He says he has balanced budgets and worked on border security, and that he is running so Wisconsin families can still own a business and raise children.
  • He says he will stand up to unelected bureaucrats and fight for farmers and other workers.
  • He argues that another four years of the current leadership in Madison would raise taxes and shrink opportunity.

tomtiffany.com

David Crowley

Democratic

Milwaukee County executive and former state representative running for governor.

His campaign says he grew up with a father who worked as an electrician and a mother who worked in the service industry, both while dealing with addiction, and that the family faced eviction. It says he and his wife are raising three daughters in Milwaukee.

  • He says he would take a storefront-revitalization program statewide, tie infrastructure spending to local hiring and labor standards, and expand apprenticeships in the trades and health care.
  • He proposes more affordable housing, universal early childhood education, and closing the state's broadband gap.
  • He says he would protect voting rights and push for nonpartisan redistricting.

Attorney General

The attorney general is the state's chief legal officer. Which cases the office files, and whether it prosecutes crimes, depends on that state's law. Wisconsin statute says the Department of Justice appears for the state and prosecutes or defends cases in the court of appeals and the supreme court in which the state is a party, and takes on other duties the law assigns.

Eric Toney

Republican

Fond du Lac County district attorney running for attorney general.

His campaign says he has prosecuted human trafficking, homicide, sexual assault, drug trafficking, and financial crimes, and that he is a past president of the Wisconsin District Attorneys Association. It says he was named the state's district attorney of the year in 2024.

  • He says he would back police, keep violent offenders off the street, and put public safety ahead of politics.
  • He says he would go after cartels and dealers tied to fentanyl and meth.
  • He says he would modernize the state crime labs and clear the testing backlog.

erictoney.com

Josh Kaul

Democratic

Wisconsin attorney general and former federal prosecutor.

His campaign says he grew up in Oshkosh and Fond du Lac and has led the state Department of Justice since 2019.

  • He says the department has investigated homicides, sexual assaults, internet crimes against children, and drug trafficking, and has sought money from companies tied to the opioid epidemic.
  • He says he supports wider background checks on firearm transfers and more funding for crime-victim services and school safety.
  • He says he is pro-choice, points to a Wisconsin Supreme Court ruling on an old abortion law, and says the office has joined cases against federal funding cuts and weaker environmental rules.

Secretary of State

The secretary of state runs the duties this state assigns that office. Those duties are not the same in every state. Wisconsin's constitution says the secretary of state keeps a record of the official acts of the legislature and the executive department and performs other duties assigned by law.

Jay Schroeder

Republican

Wisconsin native and business graduate running for secretary of state.

His campaign says he was born and raised in Clintonville, earned a business degree from UW-Stout, and was the first student elected to the Menomonie City Council.

  • He says Wisconsin should move election administration from the Wisconsin Elections Commission to the secretary of state, by legislation or by referendum.
  • He says voters can hold an elected secretary accountable for how elections are run in a way they cannot hold an appointed commission.
  • He says the office should also stand for transparency in public records and in the land funds it oversees.

JoCasta Zamarripa

Democratic

Milwaukee alder and former state representative running for secretary of state.

Her campaign says she was born and raised on Milwaukee's south side, served ten years in the Assembly, and is in her second term on the Common Council. It says she was the first Latina and the first openly bisexual member of both bodies.

  • She is using the campaign to call for state investment in affordable child care, housing, and relief on grocery costs.
  • She says the secretary of state sits on the Board of Commissioners of Public Lands and that she would protect the trust funds and land that support public schools and libraries.
  • She says she would push the state to fund public schools so districts rely less on local referenda.
