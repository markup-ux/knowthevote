Brazil · Presidential election, second round · 25 October 2026

Brazil has counted a vote for president.

Voters chose one president for the whole country. This map is not the vote for a governor, a senator, or a city council.

Brazil has 26 states and one federal district. A state is a large region. The federal district is the capital's own area. The color is who is ahead in the presidential votes the Superior Electoral Court reported for that place.

The court does not publish a color. Red in the legend means Lula is ahead. Blue means Flávio Bolsonaro is ahead. The colors only tell the two people apart.

A municipality is a city or town inside a state. Those smaller places are not drawn.
