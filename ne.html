Governor

The governor signs or vetoes bills, proposes the state budget, and appoints executive officials. The state constitution vests the supreme executive power in the governor, who shall take care that the laws are faithfully executed and that the affairs of the state are efficiently and economically administered.

Jim Pillen

Republican

Governor of Nebraska.

His campaign says he is back at work after a horseback accident and that the next stretch is about commonsense conservative solutions. He says the state's overall tax burden is lower than when he took office.

  • He says the next term should keep a focus on protecting children.
  • He says he will promote Nebraska agriculture and defend what he calls Nebraska values.
  • He says he will keep leading efforts to fix property taxes.

Senate

The winner is one of 100 votes on federal law, spending, treaties, and judges. The term is six years. The U.S. Senate has the sole power to conduct impeachment trials.

Pete Ricketts

Republican

U.S. senator and former governor of Nebraska.

His campaign says that as governor he cut taxes, made government work better, and defended Nebraska values. It says he has carried that same approach into the Senate.

  • He says he stood with President Trump to cut taxes.
  • He says he has worked to secure the border.
  • He says he is trying to stop spending he calls out of control.

State · Vote for one

Attorney General

The attorney general is the state's chief legal officer. Which cases the office files, and whether it prosecutes crimes, depends on that state's law. The office says it serves the citizens of Nebraska and the state's elected officials with fidelity to the United States Constitution, the Nebraska Constitution, and Nebraska law.

Mike Hilgers

Republican

Attorney general of Nebraska.

His campaign says he is the state's 33rd attorney general, and that before that he started a law firm, served six years in the Legislature, and was elected Speaker in 2021. He says he was born and raised in Nebraska.

  • He says the office will fight federal overreach and protect constitutional rights, including the First and Second Amendments.
  • He says he will protect children, stand with law enforcement, and defend what he calls innocent life.
  • He says he will keep communities safe and take on predatory companies, fraudsters, and scam artists.

State · Vote for one

Secretary of State

The secretary of state runs the duties this state assigns that office. Those duties are not the same in every state. The office is responsible for elections, business registrations, occupational licensing, state rules and regulations, and records management, and it keeps the Great Seal of Nebraska.

Scott Petersen

Republican

Business owner running for secretary of state.

His campaign calls him a business owner and a conservative and says he is running to restore confidence in elections and in government services. He says the office should safeguard elections, protect records, and serve businesses.

  • He says every legal vote should count, and only legal votes, through systems that can be audited.
  • He says he would strengthen the chain of custody for ballots and expand audits after the election.
  • He says business services should be efficient and should save taxpayer money.
