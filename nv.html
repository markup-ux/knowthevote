Governor

The governor signs or vetoes bills, proposes the state budget, and appoints executive officials. The supreme executive power of Nevada is vested in the governor, who is commander-in-chief of the state's military forces.

Joe Lombardo

Republican

Nevada's governor, running for another term.

Joe Lombardo's campaign says he grew up in a military family, graduated from Rancho High School and UNLV, served in the Army and the Nevada National Guard, and spent 26 years with the Las Vegas police before two terms as Clark County sheriff.

  • He says that as governor he has cut regulation, made large investments in education, and pushed accountability in schools.
  • He says he has backed law enforcement and worked on the housing shortage.
  • He says the unfinished work is a safer, stronger, and more prosperous state.

Joe Lombardo for Governor · Donate

Aaron Ford

Democratic

Nevada's attorney general, running for governor.

Aaron Ford's campaign says he grew up in a working-class family, taught public-school math, earned five degrees, and serves as attorney general. It says he and his wife, Berna, moved to Nevada.

  • He says he would speed housing permits, limit corporate buying of single-family homes, and ban software used to set rents.
  • He says pharmacy benefit managers should pass drug-discount savings to patients, and that he would set up relief for medical debt.
  • He says data centers should bring their own power, grid equipment, and water before they receive tax breaks.

Lt. Governor

The lieutenant governor is next in line if the governor cannot finish the term. Any other duties are set by state law. In Nevada, the lieutenant governor presides over the State Senate and chairs the Commission on Tourism.

Stavros Anthony

Republican

Nevada's lieutenant governor, running for another term.

Stavros Anthony's campaign says he was elected lieutenant governor in 2022 after 13 years on the Las Vegas City Council and nearly 30 years with Las Vegas police, retiring as a captain.

  • He says the job includes presiding over the Senate, chairing tourism and outdoor recreation, and serving on economic development and transportation boards.
  • He says he created a 2025 task force to protect female athletes and fairness in high school and college sports.
  • He says he advises the governor on homeland security and public safety and supports resources for local police.

Stavros Anthony for Lt. Governor · Donate

Sandra Jauregui

Democratic

State legislator running for lieutenant governor.

Sandra Jauregui says she worked during the Great Recession to help Nevadans stay in their homes and has served in the Legislature. She says she survived the October 1 mass shooting.

  • She says her legislative work includes more affordable housing, a cap on insulin costs, lower health-care costs, and protections for wages and benefits.
  • She says the shooting led her to push gun-safety laws.
  • She says she is running for lieutenant governor to take that record statewide.

Attorney General

The attorney general is the state's chief legal officer. Which cases the office files, and whether it prosecutes crimes, depends on that state's law. Nevada law has the attorney general represent the people of the state in civil and criminal matters.

Adriana Guzmán Fralick

Republican

Attorney running for attorney general.

Adriana Guzmán Fralick's campaign says she has been chief deputy district attorney in Carson City, general counsel to former Gov. Jim Gibbons, counsel to the ethics commission, and chair of the Cannabis Compliance Board.

  • She says she would undo parts of a 2019 law that raised the felony theft threshold and eased some drug penalties.
  • She says she would restore longer sentences for repeat offenders and drug traffickers and stand with police.
  • She says she would expand help for domestic-violence survivors, pursue online predators, and prosecute fraud against taxpayers.

Adriana Guzmán Fralick for Attorney General · Donate

  • It says she backed laws aimed at AI-generated child sexual abuse images and the people who distribute them.

Secretary of State

The secretary of state runs the duties this state assigns that office. Those duties are not the same in every state. In Nevada, the secretary of state maintains the official records of the acts of the Legislature and the executive branch.

Jim Marchant

Republican

Candidate for secretary of state.

Jim Marchant says he is running for secretary of state to rebuild trust in elections and to make Nevada a better place to start a business.

  • He says he would require voter identification and proof of citizenship, replace voting machines with precinct hand counts of paper ballots, and end universal mail ballots while keeping absentee voting for military, traveling, elderly, and medically unable voters.
  • He says every voter should re-register and that Nevada should leave the Electronic Registration Information Center.
  • He says he would advocate repeal of the commerce tax and lower fees to form a business.

Jim Marchant for Secretary of State · Donate

Francisco Aguilar

Democratic

Secretary of state running for another term.

His campaign calls him Cisco Aguilar and says he is running for re-election as secretary of state.

  • He says his work rests on modernization, innovation, and transparency.
  • He says he has updated the elections system and the business-filing system so elections are fair and open and so it is easier to do business in Nevada.
