Senate

The winner is one of 100 votes on federal law, spending, treaties, and judges. The term is six years. The Constitution provides two senators from each state.

Justin Murphy

Republican

Republican candidate for U.S. Senate.

Justin Murphy is the Republican nominee. His campaign says it is dedicated to members of the armed forces and publishes a written pledge of his positions.

  • He says he wants the Pinelands kept intact and the Jersey Shore kept free of pollution and offshore wind turbines.
  • He lists parental rights, medical freedom, and Medicare for seniors among his national priorities.
  • He says he would argue conservative positions and calls the race a break from a long stretch without a Republican senator from New Jersey.

Murphy for U.S. Senate · Donate

Cory Booker

Democratic

Candidate for U.S. Senate.

Cory Booker's campaign calls him a fighter for New Jersey and says the effort is aimed at justice and opportunity.

  • The site says the campaign takes no money from federal lobbyists, corporate PACs, or executives in the fossil-fuel and pharmaceutical industries.
  • It asks supporters to give directly and says the campaign is powered by grassroots donors.

Cory Booker for Senate · Donate

Veronica Fernandez

Other

Independent candidate for U.S. Senate.

Veronica Fernandez says she has lived in New Jersey her whole life, is married to a Navy veteran and electrician, and co-owns a small commercial electrical business. She says she previously worked as an insurance underwriter.

  • She says Congress has to be cleaned up so big money and special interests stop controlling it, and that the people who created the problem will not be the ones to fix it.
  • She says she has lived with the same costs for health care and groceries, help for a child with special needs, and trying to save for retirement.
  • She says she is not a lawyer and is not connected to people in power, and that new leadership has to come from outside the circle that created the problem.

Veronica Fernandez for Senate · Donate

District 1

Damon Galdo

Republican

Union carpenter running for Congress in New Jersey's 1st District.

His campaign says he is a working husband, father, and tradesman, not a career politician, who started Americas Pulse Network and the Jersey Sure podcast after an earlier run for Congress. He says South Jersey families are working harder and falling behind.

  • He says he would cut unnecessary federal spending and regulations, support American energy, and secure the border.
  • He says he would require proof of citizenship and voter identification, and give parents more say over school curriculum.
  • He says he would expand apprenticeships, back law enforcement against fentanyl trafficking, and make health prices and prescriptions more affordable, including through health savings accounts.

votedamongaldo.com

Donald Norcross

Democratic

Electrician and U.S. representative for New Jersey's 1st District.

His campaign says he is an electrician who attended community college and a skilled apprenticeship, and that he raised his first son as a single father. He says he has relied on unemployment and disability insurance, has a son in the military, and is now a grandfather.

  • He says he supports higher wages, jobs in skilled trades, and fair pay and safe workplaces.
  • He says students need affordable education that does not leave them in debt, and that every American should have affordable health care, including reproductive freedom.
  • He says he will protect Social Security and Medicare and keep seeking South Jersey funding for water, clean energy, and community projects.

donaldnorcrossforcongress.com

District 2

Jeff Van Drew

Republican

U.S. representative for New Jersey's 2nd District.

His campaign says he is focused on middle-class families and retirees in South Jersey, and that health-care costs remain one of his top priorities.

  • He says he is working to lower health-care costs and everyday expenses, including energy bills and delivery fees.
  • He says he will protect veterans' benefits, back law enforcement, and keep investing in South Jersey beaches, aviation, and infrastructure.
  • He says he supports border enforcement and proof of citizenship plus photo identification for federal voting, and that he will not back cuts to Social Security, Medicare, or Medicaid for people who qualify.

vandrewforcongress.com

Zack Mullock

Democratic

Mayor of Cape May running for Congress in New Jersey's 2nd District.

His campaign says he was born and raised in South Jersey, built a business in Cape May, and is a father of three serving a second term as the city's mayor. He says he is running as an independent voice for working families.

  • He says he would create good-paying jobs and support small businesses.
  • He says he is fighting for affordable health care and lower prescription costs.
  • He says he wants to address housing costs so working families can stay in their communities.

mullockforcongress.com

Michael McGuire

Republican

Marine and Navy reservist running for Congress in New Jersey's 3rd District.

His campaign says he is a husband, father, and veteran who served twice as a Marine and now serves in the Naval Reserve. He says he is not a politician and is running to put service ahead of self-interest.

  • He says his first priority is affordability: cutting wasteful spending and lowering taxes so working families can keep more of what they earn.
  • He says he wants people to feel safe at home and wants government held accountable.
  • He says he fully supports a ban on stock trading by members of Congress and term limits.

Herb Conaway

Democratic

Physician and U.S. representative for New Jersey's 3rd District.

His campaign says he is a lifelong Burlington County resident, the son of a teacher and a nurse, who earned degrees from Princeton, Jefferson Medical College, and Rutgers Law School. He says he served 25 years in the Assembly, including as health committee chair, before his first term in Congress.

  • He says government does not belong in the exam room and that he is fighting to keep health care, including abortion, affordable.
  • He says he wants safe communities, strong public schools, good jobs, and a healthy environment.
  • He says he has worked to ease taxes on middle-class families, protect small businesses, and open manufacturing pathways for students.

District 4

Christopher Smith

Republican

U.S. representative for New Jersey's 4th District.

His campaign says he has served 46 years and is running again after Ocean and Monmouth County Republican conventions endorsed him. It says he is known for constituent work and for turning local problems into national law.

  • He says his laws cover veterans' health care and benefits, autism, Lyme disease, Alzheimer's, and international parental child abduction.
  • He says he wrote the Trafficking Victims Protection Act and later anti-trafficking laws, and that he is worried crowded events such as the World Cup raise trafficking risks.
  • He says he backed the Working Families Tax Cut and has fought offshore wind projects he says endanger the Jersey coast.

smith4nj.com

  • She says people belong in power so families can go from surviving to thriving.

  • He says he wants to preserve what he calls immigrant-American values and protect elderly and disabled people.

  • He says voters sent him to Congress to fight for lower taxes and what he calls Jersey values.
  • He says he works across party lines to find common ground rather than follow a national political agenda.

Navy helicopter pilot and health-care executive running for Congress in New Jersey's 7th District.

Joe Hathaway

Republican

Mayor of Randolph running for Congress in New Jersey's 11th District.

His campaign says he grew up in a middle-class family in Clifton, played football at Yale, and worked in government and corporate communications, including as an aide to former Governor Chris Christie. He says he joined the Randolph council in 2022, became mayor in 2025, and lives there with his wife and three children.

Gregg Mele

Republican

Attorney and professor running for Congress in New Jersey's 12th District.

His campaign says he is a lifelong New Jersey resident raised by blue-collar parents, and that he is a businessman, attorney, and graduate professor with degrees from Montclair State, Stevens, Baruch, New York Law School, and NYU. He says he is running to restore accountability and individual liberty.

Democratic

Surgeon and Army veteran running for Congress in New Jersey's 12th District.

His campaign says he grew up working-class in Old Bridge, paid for Rutgers with an ROTC scholarship, and became an Army combat trauma surgeon after treating people at Ground Zero. He says he built a Princeton practice, has volunteered in disaster zones, and takes no corporate PAC money.

  • He says health care is a right and that he supports Medicare for All, Medicare drug-price negotiation, and a federal right to abortion.
  • He says public schools, including the ones he attended in Plainfield and Old Bridge, should be fully funded, with free school meals and tuition-free public college.
  • He says he would abolish ICE, offer a path to citizenship, and hold administrations accountable on civil rights.
