U.S. House.

Every district NBC News is publishing for this state. This list is not filtered to one address. A written note is added only from that candidate's own site.

District 1

Riley Owen

Republican

Candidate for Congress campaigning on Utah family costs and limits on political self-dealing.

His priorities page says elected officials should serve the public, and that Washington needs new faces rather than career politicians.

  • He supports tax credits for businesses that offer six weeks of paid maternity leave, and he wants federal housing funds tied to local zoning reform.
  • He says he will sponsor a bill requiring age verification for social media users 16 and older.
  • He also calls for a ban on congressional stock trading, term limits, and an end to Citizens United.

utahriley.com · Donate

Ben McAdams

Democratic

Candidate for Congress who says Utah, not a party, will set his votes.

He describes a childhood in which his mother worked as hard as anyone he knew, the lights sometimes went out, and he took his first job at 11.

  • He says his work in Congress would focus on lowering housing costs, getting people medical care, and making daily life easier for Utah families.
  • He says he will work with anyone for results and will also stand alone when he thinks democracy, protest, or due process is being trampled.
  • His priorities page also lists protecting the environment and defending human rights and opportunity.

benmcadams.com · Donate

District 2

Blake Moore

Republican

U.S. representative for northern Utah.

He says three terms in Congress have centered on costs, immigration, the military, and what he calls the Utah way of life, and he notes a constituent-service award for his office.

  • He says he is pushing border security, American energy, fiscal discipline, and conservative social policy.
  • He points to work strengthening Hill Air Force Base, expanding the charitable tax deduction, protecting the Great Salt Lake, and funding local water, roads, trails, and education.

electmoore.com · Donate

Peter Crosby

Democratic

Candidate for Congress who says northern Utah has been his home for almost 20 years.

He says he and his wife Amanda raised their family there, and he holds town halls every month.

  • He wants to confront housing costs, health care costs, and the local jobs and small businesses that hold towns together.
  • He supports a ban on congressional stock trades, a real debate on term limits, and getting corporate dark money out of politics.
  • He says he will protect public lands, work on the Great Salt Lake, and oppose corporate projects such as the Stratos data center in Box Elder County.

petercrosbyforcongress.org · Donate

District 3

Celeste Maloy

Republican

U.S. representative campaigning on taxes, the border, and federal spending.

Her campaign says she has made it a priority to slow inflation so a paycheck covers more, and she highlights a tax bill she says cut taxes on tips, overtime, and Social Security.

  • She says she voted to fund a border wall and to give Border Patrol more technology against drugs and trafficking.
  • She says she will defend religious practice, the Second Amendment, and legal protections for the unborn.
  • She says she has voted to cut wasteful spending and to ease rules she believes have held back American energy production.

celesteforutah.com · Donate

Kent Udell

Democratic

Mechanical engineer running for Utah's 3rd District.

He was born in Lehi, worked with mining, petroleum, geothermal, and solar industries, and says much of his career was spent on water, environmental cleanup, and teaching engineers.

  • He says he is running because Washington has grown more divisive and disconnected, and he wants a representative who honors the Constitution.
  • He calls for a moratorium on new data centers and says his priorities include Utah's land and water, Social Security, and the cost of health care.
  • He says he takes no corporate PAC money and has pledged to limit his own term.

kentudellforcongress.com · Donate

District 4

Mike Kennedy

Republican

U.S. representative who previously served in Utah's part-time legislature.

His campaign says he ran to follow the Constitution, rein in federal overreach, and stand up for families dealing with higher costs.

  • He says he has backed ending taxes on tips and overtime, strengthening the child tax credit, and expanding health savings accounts.
  • His campaign lists work on wildfire prevention, rail safety, energy permitting, technical education, and rural health access.
  • He says he will keep opposing spending he believes drives up family costs.

mikekennedyforutah.com · Donate

Jonny Larsen

Democratic

Marine veteran and Blue Dog Democrat running for Utah's 4th District.

He enlisted at 17, served as an infantry rifleman from 2000 to 2004, was in Washington during the attack on the Pentagon, and deployed twice to Iraq.

  • He says veterans in the district drive hours for basic care and that VA staffing cuts, including nurses and doctors, need to be reversed.
  • He credits his grandmother's Teamster pension, Medicare, and Social Security with giving her stability, and he says he will defend those programs.
  • He says there is no VA clinic in Tooele, Sevier, Millard, or Sanpete counties, in a district that includes Camp Williams, Dugway Proving Ground, and the Tooele Army Depot.

jonnyutah4congress.com · Donate
