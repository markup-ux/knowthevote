State · Vote for one

Governor

The governor signs or vetoes bills, proposes the state budget, and appoints executive officials. The South Carolina Constitution says the governor and lieutenant governor are elected jointly, so each voter casts a single vote for both, and that the governor shall take care that the laws be faithfully executed.

Alan Wilson

Republican

South Carolina attorney general and Army National Guard colonel

Alan Wilson's campaign says he joined the South Carolina National Guard after college, has served since 1996, and deployed during Operation Iraqi Freedom. It says he is the state's attorney general and is running for governor.

  • He highlights child-predator prosecutions, a larger Internet Crimes Against Children task force, and the state's human-trafficking task force.
  • He says he has gone to court against policies of the Obama and Biden administrations, including what he calls a transgender agenda.
  • He says he backs President Trump on immigration enforcement and election integrity.

Jermaine Johnson

Democratic

State representative running for governor

Jermaine Johnson's campaign says he is a state representative who has passed bills on the senior sales tax, a closed bridge, and broadband. The site also introduces Sam Skardon as his lieutenant-governor running mate.

  • He says he would expand the number of families who owe no income tax rather than eliminate the tax, because he says ending it would mainly help the wealthiest, and he says he would work with cities to cut property taxes.
  • He says he would let cities fix roads with transportation-department money, raise law-enforcement pay, and fund more rural hospitals and trade schools.
  • He says he would audit public education, protect reproductive decisions for patients and doctors, and legalize medical marijuana while putting recreational use to a statewide vote.

Senate

The winner is one of 100 votes on federal law, spending, treaties, and judges. The term is six years.

Darline Graham

Republican

U.S. senator

Darline Graham's campaign says she was appointed to the Senate by Governor Henry McMaster after her brother, Senator Lindsey Graham, died. It says she spent nearly 30 years in state vocational rehabilitation and led the Commission for the Blind.

  • She says she would make the Trump tax cuts permanent, cut waste and fraud, and back workforce training, the skilled trades, and work requirements for able-bodied adults.
  • She says she supports finishing a border wall, ending catch-and-release, and opposing amnesty and taxpayer-funded benefits for people here illegally.
  • She says she opposes taxpayer funding of abortion, supports voter identification and current voter rolls, and opposes gun registries and red-flag confiscation.

  • Her listed plans include tackling debt and congressional perks, cutting taxes, lowering premiums, protecting rural hospitals, and reversing Medicaid cuts.
  • The platform also lists strong schools and affordable child care, reproductive freedom, political corruption, and an end to gun violence.

Attorney General

The attorney general is the state's chief legal officer. Which cases the office files, and whether it prosecutes crimes, depends on that state's law. The South Carolina Constitution says the attorney general assists and represents the governor in seeing that the laws are faithfully executed, and may not use that power against the General Assembly or the Supreme Court.

David Stumbo

Republican

Prosecutor running for attorney general

David Stumbo's campaign calls him a career criminal prosecutor and the upstate's top prosecutor. He says he is running to be attorney general.

  • He says he would defend the Constitution, religious liberty, parental rights, and what he calls the sanctity of life of the unborn.
  • He says he has prosecuted people in the country illegally, murderers, rapists, domestic abusers, and drug dealers, and that he would keep doing that work on the first day in office.
  • He says he supports law enforcement, health-care freedom, lower taxes, and a more transparent government.

Secretary of State

The secretary of state runs the duties this state assigns that office. Those duties are not the same in every state. The South Carolina Constitution says the secretary of state countersigns commissions sealed with the Great Seal, and that the office's other duties are prescribed by law.

Mark Hammond

Republican

Candidate for South Carolina secretary of state.

His campaign says South Carolina businesses are growing and that the secretary of state's office has made filings easier. He asks voters to share that record as the campaign continues.

  • He says Business One Stop has made it easier for businesses to expand in the state.
  • He credits the High Growth Small Business Job Creation Act with drawing angel investment into early-stage, high-growth companies.
  • He says oversight of charities is part of why the state ranks in the top five nationally for charitable giving.

Jim Clyburn

Democratic

U.S. representative for South Carolina's 6th District since 1993.

His official biography says he grew up in Sumter as the son of a minister and a beautician, graduated from South Carolina State, and taught school in Charleston before becoming the first Black advisor to a South Carolina governor and later Human Affairs commissioner. He is a former House majority whip and now ranking member of an Appropriations subcommittee.

  • He says his 10-20-30 formula sends at least 10 percent of certain funds to communities where 20 percent or more of people have lived in poverty for 30 years.
  • He points to his Rural Energy Savings Program, which lets rural electric cooperatives lend for home energy efficiency, and to a broadband bill funded in the 2021 infrastructure law.
  • His biography says he has worked to make America's greatness accessible and affordable, including preserving historic buildings at historically Black colleges and creating heritage corridors and parks.
