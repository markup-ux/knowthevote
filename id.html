State · Vote for one

Governor

The governor signs or vetoes bills, proposes the state budget, and appoints executive officials. The governor is commander-in-chief of the state's military forces, except when they are called into actual service of the United States, and is chairman of the State Board of Land Commissioners.

Brad Little

Republican

Governor of Idaho, first elected in 2018 and reelected in 2022.

His campaign says he is committed to leading so Idahoans can thrive, so children and grandchildren choose to stay, and so people who have left choose to come back. The site calls that experienced leadership the state can trust.

  • His campaign says he cut or simplified 95 percent of Idaho regulations and calls Idaho the least regulated state.
  • His campaign says teacher pay rose, literacy funding increased five-fold, and school-facility spending also worked as property-tax relief, along with new school-choice options and the Idaho LAUNCH program.
  • His campaign says he and the Legislature delivered tax relief with a balanced budget and a AAA credit rating, and that Idaho sent more missions to the border than any other state through an effort he calls Operation No Return.

Candidate for governor of Idaho.

  • She says growth should rest on small business, a stable tax structure, a living wage, and health care that is affordable.
  • She says she would repeal the school-voucher program, raise teacher pay, and fund rural districts, pre-K, and full-day kindergarten.
  • She says public lands must stay public and open for recreation, hunting, and fishing, and that women, not the state, should make family-planning decisions.

Libertarian

State · Vote for one

Lt. Governor

The lieutenant governor is next in line if the governor cannot finish the term. Any other duties are set by state law. The Idaho Constitution makes the lieutenant governor president of the Senate, with a vote only when the Senate is equally divided.

Scott Bedke

Republican

Lieutenant governor, cattle rancher, and former Speaker of the Idaho House.

He says his family ranching in the Oakley Basin goes back to the 1870s. He was born in Twin Falls, graduated from Oakley High School, and started in public service on the Oakley City Council.

  • He says he has an A+ rating from the NRA and a 100 percent pro-life record with Idaho Chooses Life.
  • He says he led a fight to keep critical race theory out of Idaho schools.
  • He says he was named legislator of the year by the Fraternal Order of Police and will oppose defunding the police, work to secure the border, and keep families safe.

Federal · Vote for one

Senate

The winner is one of 100 votes on federal law, spending, treaties, and judges. The term is six years. Senate committees gather information through investigations and hearings in order to recommend legislation, and they evaluate presidential nominees for executive and judicial posts.

Jim Risch

Republican

U.S. senator from Idaho seeking another term.

He is a small-business owner, a farmer and beef producer, and a former county prosecuting attorney. He says that as governor he cut property taxes by 20 percent, and that he is a former chairman of the Senate Small Business Committee.

  • He says he led the Senate fight against selling some of Idaho's public lands and will keep defending multiple use, including hunting, logging, and grazing.
  • He says he will keep cutting taxes and red tape for small businesses and farms, and he cites a 100 percent approval rating from the American Farm Bureau.
  • He says he supports border enforcement, Second Amendment rights, and returning natural-resource decisions to state and local leaders.

State · Vote for one

Attorney General

The attorney general is the state's chief legal officer. Which cases the office files, and whether it prosecutes crimes, depends on that state's law. Idaho law assigns the attorney general the duty to represent the state and its agencies in court and, when the public service requires it, to assist a county prosecuting attorney.

Raul Labrador

Republican

Idaho attorney general.

The campaign site identifies him as Attorney General Raúl Labrador and says the campaign is about protecting Idaho families. It invites supporters to watch an ad titled as a fight for Idaho.

  • The site's headline says he is protecting Idaho families.
  • It highlights his defense of women's sports at the U.S. Supreme Court.

State · Vote for one

Secretary of State

The secretary of state runs the duties this state assigns that office. Those duties are not the same in every state. Idaho law charges the secretary of state with custody of legislative acts and resolutions, the legislative journals, and the great seal.

Incumbent who says he has overseen Idaho elections and modernized services for voters and businesses.

He says that since taking office he has worked to strengthen elections, expand transparency, and modernize services for voters and businesses. He says he oversaw the largest election in Idaho history.

  • He says he trained as a wildland firefighter and works with timber, mining, agriculture, and recreation on public-land challenges.
  • He says he will protect faith, family, personal responsibility, and limited government.
